FIELD GUIDE / 028

Every turn
leaves a trace.

This instrument composes right-handed X, Y and Z rotations as 3×3 matrices. Order matters: XYZ computes Q ← Q Rₓ Rᵧ Rz, while ZYX computes Q ← Q Rz Rᵧ Rₓ.

Orthogonality drift is the Frobenius distance ‖QᵀQ − I‖. Precision stress rounds each update to six decimals; Gram–Schmidt rebuilds an orthonormal right-handed frame from the columns.
